# Workflow and User Journey

Follow one concrete case from trigger to completion. Establish actors, inputs, steps, decisions, handoffs, permissions, waiting states, and the final result. Then probe consequential alternate paths: cancellation, missing input, rejection, retries, and recovery. Distinguish today's workflow from the intended future one.

Useful probes: “Who acts next, and how do they know?” “What happens if nobody responds?” “Can this step be reversed?” Ask about automation versus human judgment when it changes the workflow.

Close with a concise sequence or state/transition outline, actor responsibilities, and important exceptions. Do not turn a simple journey into an exhaustive process model or assume every manual step should be automated.
