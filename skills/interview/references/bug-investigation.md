# Bug Investigation

Reuse logs, screenshots, tests, and supplied reproduction steps. Ask about missing expected behavior, observed result, smallest reproduction, frequency, environment, affected data, recent changes, and impact only where they narrow diagnosis. Distinguish observed facts from suspected causes.

Useful probes: “What did you expect immediately after that action?” “Does it happen with a new record too?” “What is the last step that consistently works?” Use non-destructive diagnostics; do not request secrets or destructive resets.

Close with expected versus actual behavior, reproduction or explicit reproduction gap, environment evidence, and success criterion. Label hypotheses. Specialized debugging and verification own diagnosis and proof; an interview alone cannot establish root cause or a verified fix.
